> Mongoose is an Object Data Modeling (ODM) library for MongoDB Docs and Node.js. It acts as a translator or a structured bridge between raw MongoDB collections and your JavaScript code. While MongoDB is naturally "schema-less" (allowing documents in the same collection to have entirely different structures), Mongoose introduces a predictable, schema-based layer to your application
